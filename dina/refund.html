Refund policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

We want you to feel safe when you book a reading or buy a course. Here is exactly when and how a refund works.

Private readings

  • If you cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled call, you receive a full refund.
  • If you cancel less than 24 hours before the call, or once the consultation has taken place, the payment is not refunded. For readings with several sessions, this applies from the first session.

Voice note readings

A voice note reading is prepared individually for you. Before it is sent, you can cancel for a full refund. Once the voice note has been sent, the payment is not refunded.

Recorded courses

Before you receive access, you can cancel for a full refund. Once access to the recording has been sent, the payment is not refunded, because the content has been delivered in full.

Live courses

Refund terms for each live group are announced together with its dates. Joining the waitlist is free.

How to request a refund

Write to dinazoa44@gmail.com or to WhatsApp support with your name, the service and the date of payment. Approved refunds are returned to the card used for payment within 10 business days. The time it takes for the money to appear on your card depends on your bank.
